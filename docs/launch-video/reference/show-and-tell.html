what buddy does · 1 of 4

Talks with you

Hears “hey buddy”

Heard on the Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere until you speak.

Answers on its screen

Captions appear under its eyes, with beeps and boops.

Searches the web

Weather, scores or prices, in one short line.

Thinks hard

Math, code and planning questions go to a slower, stronger model.

Remembers your talks

A few lines per chat, never the words. “Remember that” keeps a fact for good.

Hears “bye” and “be quiet”

It ends the chat, or goes silent and keeps moving.

what buddy does · 2 of 4

Uses your computer

Does tasks on your Mac

“Open Spotify and play something.”

It clicks and types, up to 25 steps. It asks before it sends, pays or deletes anything. Say “stop” at any time.

Keeps you company while you code

It naps when Claude Code is idle and looks busy while it works. When a session needs you, it searches the room for you. Tap it to jump to that window.

Takes meeting notes

Start it from the Mac. It writes down what's said, then lists the decisions, the to-dos and the open questions.

what buddy does · 3 of 4

Sees and moves

Follows your face

It turns to look at you, learns where you usually sit, and knows you from a stranger.

Looks where you ask

“What do you see?” “Look left.” “Where's my mug?”

Dances

Sway, bob, nod, wiggle, figure-8. For now you start a dance from the Mac, not by voice.

Loves a head pat

Pat its head and its lights beat pink while it chirps happily.

what buddy does · 4 of 4

Has a life of its own

Explores

After 10 quiet minutes, or when you say “go explore,” it looks around the room.

Has moods

Curious, happy, bored, lonely, startled. You can see it in its eyes, lights, head and chirps.

Keeps a diary

It writes down only new or interesting thoughts, and at night it sleeps on the day.

Takes photos of cool things

On its own judgment. At most 2 an hour and 8 a day.

Shares a thought

Now and then a thought appears on its screen, no more than one every 4 minutes.

Lives in a widget

A desktop widget shows its mood. The diary app has 7 tabs, from Talking to Dreams.

new for kids
the kid

Stuck halfway through a problem

They want help right at the line where they got lost. They don't want a lecture, and they don't want to start over.

the grown-up

Wants the kid to do the thinking

They want a helper that won't do the homework. buddy's help is always small, and its writing looks different from the kid's.

designed
three kinds of help

Small help, one piece at a time

Give me a hint

“What do both sides have in common?”

A nudge in the right direction. It never finishes a step.

Check my work

  1. Can I read it?
  2. Are the steps right?
  3. Is it finished?

If something is wrong, it explains the first slip only.

Show one step

“Here's the next line, and why.”

Exactly one step, written so it looks different from the kid's work. Tap again for the next one.

The whole answer is never on the menu.
